Bulk World Operations

Sets every cost quantity in this world to 1 (emeralds, items). Max stock and uses remain unchanged.

Resets traded count to 0 on all offers across all villagers, immediately unlocking locked trades.

Set Max Stock (9999 Uses)

Sets maximum uses on all trades to 9999 so offers rarely lock out.
